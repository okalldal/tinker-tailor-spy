# TuxedoShirt

**Delivery:** 2–3 weeks


## Customization Options


### Collar

- **Wing Tip** (`MTCT9`) — The classic black-tie style, tuxedo shirt collars typically feature small, spread points that allow for a bowtie to be shown fully and unencumbered.
- **Classic** (`CCC1`) — This versatile collar features a short spread and classic sharp points.
- **High Widespread** (`CCWS2`) — Like the classic wide collar, this style offers ample space between subtly outwardly oriented points but carries a slightly shorter collar length.

### Placket

- **Hidden Button Placket** (`PSHP4`) — Often found on tux shirts, hidden placket features a layer of fabric folded over to hide the buttons.
- **Pique Bib** (`TPSTPIQ1`) — Finished in a special pique weave, these smart tuxedo shirts bring the perfect blend of traditional elegance and subtly textured style to any black-tie look.
- **Pleated Bib** (`TPSTPL2`) — Pleated bib —a narrow textured ribbed design—brings the perfect blend of modern elegance and subtly textured style to any black-tie look.

### Buttons

- **Tuxedo Stud Black** (`BC13`)
- **White Flat** (`B03`)
- **Tuxedo Stud White Mother Of Pearl** (`BC17`) *(+€10)*
- **Black** (`B17`)

### Cuff

- **Single Cuff One Button** (`CSR1`) — A classic single cuff with a single button closure and rounded edges.
- **Single Chisel** (`CSS2`) — Formal & business-ready squared cuffs feature a broad angled closure.
- **Single Boat Adjustable** (`CSBA`) — The single-boat adjustable cuff has a practical second button running parallel to the cuff edge, allowing you to adjust the tightness of the cuff around the wrist, or open it altogether for a casual appeal.
- **Double Cuff Round** (`CDR3`) — Double cuffs feature fabric folded back onto itself which must be fastened with cufflinks.
- **Double Cuff Square** (`CDS4`) — Double cuffs feature fabric folded back onto itself which must be fastened with cufflinks.

### Handmade gathers *(advanced)*

- **No Handmade Gathers** (`SSLSS1`) — A classic sleeve creates a smooth, streamlined arm that work well for any style and occasion.
- **Pleated Shoulder** (`SSLSP4`) *(+€10)* — Pleated sleeves feature small pleats along the shoulder—ideal for more formal or business styles.
- **Pleated Shoulder & Cuff** (`SSLSP2`) *(+€10)* — Pleated sleeves feature small pleats along the shoulder and cuff—ideal for more formal or business styles.
- **Pleated Shoulder, Cuff & Back** (`SSLSP8`) *(+€10)* — Small pleats along the shoulder, cuff & back—ideal for more formal or business styles.

### Hem shape *(advanced)*

- **Standard** (`HST`) — A formal style characterized by extended front and back hems, tailed shirts are made to be easily tucked.
