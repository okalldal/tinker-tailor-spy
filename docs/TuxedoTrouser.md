## Customization Options


### Waistband


#### Waistband

- **Extended Straight** (`WBBR5`) — A sleek, waistband with a hidden button and single belt loop for securing the extended closure. Found on our Brentwood trouser
- **Standard Hidden button** (`WBWA6`) — Our classic waistband with a single hidden button under a squared shaped closure
- **Single Side closure** (`WBFE8`) — A clean, featureless front, using instead a buckle closure on the right hip of the waistband. Found on our Fellini trouser.
- **Double Side closure** (`WBGU9`) — This military-inspired style features a distinct adjustable buckles on both hips and a full extension across the front.

#### Details

- **Belt Loops** (`WBBL1`) — Classic yet versatile, finished for wearing with or without a belt
- **None** (`WBNO3`) — No side adjusters or belt loops for a clean, minimalist finish
- **Long Side Adjusters** (`WBLSA5`) — This variant features buckles on either hip on the waistband, but with extended fabric at the back end for a small style detail.
- **Winged Side Adjusters** (`WBWSA6`) — This "winged" variant features buckles on either hip on the waistband, but with extended, pointed, fabric at the back end for a small style detail.
- **Single Closure Buckle** (`WBCSA1`) — Single Closure Buckle as found on our Fellini trouser
- **Double Closure Buckle** (`WBCSA2`) — Double Closure Buckle as found on our Gurkha trouser

### Pocket


#### Side

- **Slanted** (`FPSL1`) — Positioned between the waist and side seam of a pair of trousers, slanted pockets are perhaps the most common style of pocket, and are practical and versatile enough for casual and more formal options.
- **Jetted** (`FPJ2`) — Formal & exclusively aesthetic, jetted pockets are defined by a fine trimmed slit cut into the fabric.
- **Seam** (`FPSE3`) — The pocket is built into the side seams. They provide a clean, streamlined silhouette.

#### Back

- **Jetted with Button** (`BPJ1`) — A timeless trouser feature granting a classic, tailored look.
- **Jetted No Button** (`BPJNOB2`) — The jetted rear pocket, crafted without buttons, offers a sleek, elegant look ideal for black-tie styles.
- **Flap with Button** (`BPFWB3`) — A sportier take on the rear pocket, providing a more relaxed appeal.

#### Coin

- **Hidden Coin Pocket** (`CPWNF03`) — A small coin pocket hidden from view
- **No Coin Pocket** (`CPWN01`)

### Pleat

- **None** (`PLNO1`) — Non-pleated trousers, known as flat fronted, lie flat against the leg for a neat, versatile appeal.
- **Standard Single Pleat** (`PLS2`) — The standard single pleat opens towards the side seam.
- **Standard Double Pleat** (`PLD3`) — Double standard pleats opens towards the side seam.
- **Inward Single Pleat** (`PLRS4`) — A single inward pleat opens towards the crotch.
- **Inward Double Pleat** (`PLRD5`) — Double inward pleats opens towards the crotch.

### Hem Finishing

- **Plain** (`THFP1`) — Plain hem
- **Unfinished** (`THFUF4`) — Unfinished hems offer 11 extra centimeters of fabric, allowing you to finish the trousers your way.
