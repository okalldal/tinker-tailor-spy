### Lapel


#### Lapel

- **Notch** (`LN1`) — The most standard and versatile lapel works well for any occasion.
- **Concave** (`LNH3`) — A slightly widened notch lapel that features a subtle curvature.
- **Peak** (`LP2`) — Peak lapels broaden the shoulders and are more angular with a bold upward peak.
- **Straight Peak** (`LP4`) — Provides a sharp, strong aesthetic that broadens the shoulders.
- **Low Gorge Notch** (`LGN5`) — This low-gorge notch lapel is a refined, versatile style that creates a broader, more well-defined upper body.
- **Low Gorge Peak** (`LGP6`) — The lower gorge on this peak lapel is ideal for creating an overall more relaxed look without losing the bold appeal.

#### Width

- **Narrow** (`NLWN1`) — This Narrow lapel width measures in at 8.6cm.

- **Standard** (`LNLWS2`) — The standard width of our Low Gorge Notch lapel measures in at 10cm.
- **Standard** (`LPLWS2`) — The standard width of our Low Gorge Peak lapel measures in at 11cm.

### Shoulder *(advanced)*

- **Natural Shirt** (`SS3`) — This natural shoulder joins the sleeve head underneath the shoulder seam for a more shirt-like fit.
- **Natural Pleated** (`SNP7`) *(+€20)* — This natural shoulder offers a softer silhouette & features small handmade pleats along the armhole seam.
- **Natural** (`SNA6`) — The highly versatile natural shoulder features minimal padding for a balanced finish.
- **Unconstructed** (`SUC4`) — Crafted without padding, an unconstructed shoulder is regarded as the most casual of styles.
- **Lightly Padded** (`SREG1`) — A traditional shoulder, lightly padded shoulder for a classic silhouette.
- **Lightly Padded Pleated** (`SP5`) *(+€20)* — This classic lightly padded shoulder features a series of small handmade pleats along the armhole seam.
- **Structured** (`SROP2`) — A structured shoulder features strong, defined shoulder line for an elegant and enhanced silhouette.

### Pocket


#### Side *(advanced)*

- **Standard Flap (5.5cm)** (`SPF5.5`) — This style features a standard 5.5cm flap of fabric over a jetted pocket which can be let out or tucked in.
- **Large Flap (6.5cm)** (`SPF7`) — This style features a large 6.5cm flap of fabric over a jetted pocket which can be let out or tucked in.
- **Jetted** (`SPJ4`) — Formal and sleek, jetted pockets are defined by a fine trimmed slit cut into the fabric.
- **Patch** (`SPPA5`) — A sportier take on the more typical flap pocket, patch pockets offer a more relaxed appeal.
- **Round Patch** (`SPRP7`) — This take on the casual patch pocket features a curved bottom.
- **Tulip Patch** (`SPTU8`) — Tulip pockets are patch pockets with a prominent curve in the opening for an added touch of flair.

#### Ticket *(advanced)*

- **Yes** (`TPY1`) — Typically a style accent, a smaller flap pocket located above the right flap pocket.
- **No** (`TPN2`) — No ticket pocket.

#### Chest *(advanced)*

- **Boat Shape** (`CPBS2`) — Named after the Italian word for "little boat", barchetta pockets are subtly curved chest pockets.
- **Patch** (`CPPA2`) — Chest patch pockets offer a more easygoing and practical alternative to more formal options.
- **Rounded Patch** (`CPRP4`) — Chest patch pockets offer a more casual alternative to more formal options—this style is rounded.

### AMF *(advanced)*

- **6mm** (`HAMF6mm3`) — Found 6mm in, along the edges of the hem, pockets and lapel to prevent fabric curling.
