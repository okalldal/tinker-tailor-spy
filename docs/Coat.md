# Coat

**Delivery:** 2–3 weeks


## Customization Options


### Closure

- **2.5 Button Single Breasted** (`CM2.5SB`) — This style features a button on the lapel roll—a purely aesthetic detail that creates a lower closure.
- **6 Button Double Breasted** (`CM6DB`) — A classic double-breasted front featuring a 6-button closure.

### Lapel

- **Notch** (`CLN1`) — Classic & versatile, the notch lapel works well in any setting.
- **Peak** (`CLP2`) — Peak lapels broaden the shoulders and are more angular with a bold upward peak.

### Lining


#### Type

- **Full Lined** (`CLFL`) — Features a linking throughout the entirety of a coat's body and arms.
- **Unlined** (`CLUN`) — Feature lining only in the sleeves, with none throughout the body.

### Belt *(advanced)*

- **No** (`CWNB`) — A finish without belt loops creates a sleeker, more streamlined silhouette.
- **Yes** (`CWBB`) — Belted coats feature a removable belt made of like fabric.

### Length *(advanced)*

- **Standard** (`CVIC0`) — The standard length of our coats, found on the Vicenza.
- **Standard +15cm** (`CDBFL15`) — Cut to +15cm longer than the standard length, as seen on the Torino.
- **Extended +25CM** (`CEXT25`) *(+€25)* — Cut to +25cm longer than our classic length coats, making for a more refined, streamlined look.

### Side Pocket *(advanced)*

- **Slanted Welt** (`CSPW`) — Slanted welted pockets are slightly angled for easier access.
- **Large Flap (7cm)** (`CSPF`) — This style features a large 7cm flap of fabric over a jetted pocket which can be let out or tucked in.
- **Patch** (`CSPP`) — Patch pockets feature a bucket-shaped piece of fabric sewn to the outside of a garment.

### Shoulder *(advanced)*

- **Natural** (`CSNA6`) — The highly versatile natural shoulder features minimal padding for a balanced finish.
- **Structured** (`CSROP2`) — A structured shoulder features strong, defined shoulder line for an elegant and enhanced silhouette.

### Amf *(advanced)*

- **2mm** (`CAMF2mm`) — Found along the edges of the hem, pockets and lapel to prevent fabric curling.
- **6mm** (`CAMF6mm`) — Found 6mm in, along the edges of the hem, pockets and lapel to prevent fabric curling.

## Style Presets


### Single Breasted

*This style features a 2.5 button closure, a notch lapel & welted pockets.*

- **Type:** Full Lined
- **Amf:** 2mm
- **Side Pocket:** Slanted Welt
- **Belt:** No
- **Closure:** 2.5 Button Single Breasted
- **Lapel:** Notch
- **Length:** Standard
- **Shoulder:** Natural

### Double Breasted

*This style features a 6-button double breasted closure, structured shoulder and flap pockets.*

- **Type:** Full Lined
- **Amf:** 2mm
- **Side Pocket:** Large Flap (7cm)
- **Belt:** No
- **Closure:** 6 Button Double Breasted
- **Lapel:** Peak
- **Shoulder:** Structured
